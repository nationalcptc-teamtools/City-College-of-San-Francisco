# Client Debrief Presentation Outline

> Audience: the client's leadership, played by judges. They care about **risk to the business**, not tools. Target length: whatever the event specifies. Rehearse it to time.

1. **Title.** Firm name (no school identifiers), client name, date.
2. **Engagement overview.** Scope, the window, what we were asked to answer. One slide.
3. **Bottom line.** The overall risk posture in one sentence, plus a severity count.
4. **The attack story.** One diagram: starting point → key steps → deepest access (finding IDs on the arrows).
5. **Top 3 risks.** One slide each: what it is, the business impact for *this* client (rides, guests, payments), and the fix.
6. **What's working.** Genuine positive observations.
7. **Remediation roadmap.** Immediate, 30-day, and 90-day columns.
8. **Incidents during testing.** Anything we disrupted, how we reported it, and the status. Owning this openly reads as professional.
9. **Questions.**

## Speaker rules
- Every team member who speaks has one clear section
- No jargon without a one-line translation
- Assign one person to answer each question (no pile-ons)
- Anticipate the three hardest questions and rehearse the answers
